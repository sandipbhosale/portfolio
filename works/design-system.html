A design system across products

EnKash · Founding Designer · Product design systems

At EnKash, product development was engineer-led and code-first. As the product portfolio grew, individual products began to look and behave differently. I helped move the team toward a structured, user-centred design lifecycle and created and maintained a multi-product design system to bring consistency across experiences.

The challenge

Products were being shaped primarily through engineering implementation, which led to inconsistent patterns and experiences across the portfolio.

My contribution

I helped introduce a more structured, user-centred design lifecycle and established a shared design system for multiple products.

Design system focus

The system provided a common foundation for reusable interface patterns and consistent product experiences.

Outcome

A shared design foundation helped the team move toward more consistent experiences and a more deliberate design process. Specific impact metrics are not included because they have not been provided.
